The Grounds

Stone path, tall pines, brick walls that go up further than they should. Nobody remembers who laid the path or why it curves the way it does.

This is where lobbies land first, before everyone splits off to go poke at something they shouldn't.

Slop Shop

Follow the path far enough and the trees part around a hand painted sign glowing over a doorway. Slop Shop trades whatever junk the manor has lying around, traffic cones included.

Nobody has ever seen who runs it. Stock changes. Prices change. The shopkeeper does not show up.

Cosmetics Wing

Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

This app collects and processes limited user data provided by the Meta platform and third party services used to operate the game. User data is not sold, shared for advertising, or used for tracking outside of gameplay functionality.

Meta Platform User ID

The app uses the Meta Platform User ID as the internal account identifier for authentication, save data, player progression, and ownership validation. Players may choose their own public in game display name, but the Meta User ID is required behind the scenes to uniquely identify and authenticate each player account.

The User ID is used to:

  • Authenticate returning players
  • Load and save player specific data
  • Restore owned cosmetics and in app purchases
  • Prevent account duplication or impersonation
  • Associate multiplayer activity with the correct account

Without access to the Meta User ID, the app would not be able to maintain persistent player accounts or securely restore player data across sessions and devices. The app does not use User ID data for advertising, profiling, or tracking.

User Age Group (Meta Platform)

Used to determine age appropriate access to features and to comply with platform safety requirements. The app does not collect exact age or date of birth.

PlayFab Player Data

PlayFab is used to track player progression, statistics, and account related game data. No real world personal information such as real name, email address, or physical address is collected by the app.

Photon Networking Data

Photon is used to provide multiplayer server connectivity. Photon processes session and connection data required for real time multiplayer gameplay.

In App Purchase and Entitlement Data

Used to verify purchases, unlock downloadable content, and restore purchased items.

How We Use The Data

The collected data is used only to enable core gameplay features, support multiplayer and online services, save player progress and game statistics, validate in app purchases and downloadable content, enforce age appropriate access to features, and comply with Meta platform policies.

Data Deletion and Account Removal

Player data associated with this app is managed through PlayFab. Uninstalling the game stops all further data collection, though stored gameplay data may remain associated with the user account unless deletion is requested.

Users may request deletion of their stored account data at any time by contacting us at cadebreaux66@example.com. Upon request, all PlayFab account data associated with the user will be permanently deleted within a reasonable timeframe, except where retention is required by platform policy or law.

Third Party Services

This app uses PlayFab for player accounts, progression, and statistics, and Photon for multiplayer server networking. These services only process data necessary to provide gameplay features and do not use the data for advertising purposes.

Children's Privacy

This app relies on Meta's User Age Group classification and does not knowingly collect personal information beyond what is provided by the platform and required for gameplay.
